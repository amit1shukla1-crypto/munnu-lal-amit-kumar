Essential Road Materials

We use industry-standard materials to ensure durability and safety.

🪨

Aggregates (Gravel)

Crushed stone of various sizes used for the base and sub-base layers to provide structural strength.

🖤

Bitumen / Asphalt

The sticky black binder that holds aggregates together, providing a waterproof and smooth surface.

⏳

Subgrade Soil

The native soil, compacted and reinforced to support the entire weight of the road structure.

🧱

Cement Concrete

Used for rigid pavements, providing extreme durability for heavy-duty industrial roads.

🌾

Geotextiles

Synthetic fabrics used for soil stabilization and drainage to prevent road erosion.

💧

Water/Additives

Controlled moisture and chemical additives to enhance the binding properties of the material.
